AN EXPERIMENT IN WORDS & TIME

The Wind Remembers

An interactive poem about memory and change

✦

The wind remembers what we forget,

The names we whispered, the promises kept.

It carries yesterday through the trees,

And leaves our secrets beneath the breeze.

Some days arrive like a quiet song,

Some nights remind us where we belong.

The stars rearrange, the shadows depart,

But time leaves fingerprints on the heart.

So let the wind turn every page,

Let moments wander beyond their cage.

For nothing stays, and nothing ends,

The world keeps moving, and the wind remembers.

✦

Words change places, but their meaning remains.

The poem is waiting for the wind...

Written in moments. Remembered by the wind.
